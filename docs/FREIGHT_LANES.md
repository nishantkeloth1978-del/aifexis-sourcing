# Freight lane tender

How the freight RFQ works. Template: **Freight lanes RFQ** (`LOG_FREIGHT_RFQ`, version 3).

## What the buyer does
1. Create an event from the template. Fill the event details (contract period, rate validity).
2. List the lanes. Either type them, or use **Download import sheet**, fill it in Excel (yellow headings are required) and **Import from Excel**. Rows are added to the table; press **Save inputs** to keep them.
3. Each lane becomes a lot named "Origin to Destination". Five price lines are made per lane: ocean freight, fuel surcharge (BAF), origin port charges, destination port charges, documents and other. Only ocean freight must be above zero; the others may be 0.
4. Publish as usual.

## What the supplier does
- On the bid page the price section is a **lane table**. Type prices, or **download the lane sheet**, fill the yellow cells and **upload** it, then check the table on screen and submit.
- Per lane the supplier also states transit days (required), sailings per week, free days and rate validity.
- A lane is either priced completely or left empty (no bid on that lane). A lane that is priced needs its transit time.
- Excel: one row per lane. Do not change the grey cells or the Lane numbers. The all-in price, annual cost and "Meets limit?" calculate in the sheet.

## What the buyer sees
- After the commercial envelopes open: **Lanes side by side** (price per carrier for every lane, lowest highlighted, transit against the limit), **Lane terms by carrier**, and **Award scenarios** (everything to one carrier, lane by lane, saving from splitting).
- The recommendation works per lot, so each lane can go to a different carrier. **Select lowest for each lane** and **Select for all lanes** fill in the choices; the buyer can still change any lane and must give the reason as usual.
- The side-by-side figures compare price only. The ranking below them also weighs the technical score.

## For developers
- Template model: a price group with `grid`, `asLot`, `answers`, `limit`, `nameFrom` (see `src/templates/types.ts`); lines can be `zeroOk`. Content: `src/templates/packs/freight.ts`; migration generated by `scripts/gen-template-seed.ts`.
- Lanes become lots in `writeSchedule` (`src/templates/events.ts`); `event_lot.template_row` marks lots made from the template so they are rebuilt with the schedule.
- Supplier answers are stored as a `lane_terms` bid item (commercial class, opened with the prices). Screen and Excel both end in the same server checks (`src/bids/lanes.ts`, `src/bids/service.ts`).
- Comparison: `src/commercial/lanes.ts` (pure), shown by `src/ui/LaneMatrixPanel.tsx`.
